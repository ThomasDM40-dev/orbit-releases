Orbit Premium

Orbit is free. Orbit Premium unlocks the most advanced AI tools, for a one-time payment of €14.99, valid for life on 1 device.

What is included

Free Premium
Downloads, Spotify, Twitch, file hosts, Subscriptions AI Generation
Converter and HandBrake AI Magic Eraser
Toolbox and Media Library AI Cutout
Audio Studio: denoise, normalize, extract AI Enhance
AI 3D Creation AI Interpolator
Paint a plate Topaz Video AI
Discord Drive Transcription
AI Assistant Audio Studio: stem separation

A Premium tool stays visible: when opened, it shows a locked screen with the button to go Premium.

Buy

  1. Click Premium at the bottom of the sidebar (or Menu › File › Go Premium).
  2. Create an account or sign in to your Orbit account.
  3. Click the buy button (€14.99): payment opens in your browser (Stripe; card, or PayPal depending on your country).
  4. Come back to Orbit: Premium unlocks by itself as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Paid another way (crypto) or received a key? Use the "Paid in crypto or received a key?" link, then activate your key.

Gift Premium

To receive Premium as a gift, with no account or payment: open the Premium window, copy your device ID and give it to the person offering it. Once confirmed, click the gift check button: Orbit activates.

Good to know

  • Premium tools run on your PC; once activated, Premium also works offline.
  • Premium is tied to one device. To move to another PC, contact support.
  • The button at the bottom of the sidebar reads Premium active when everything is unlocked.
