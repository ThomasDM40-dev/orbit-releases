What Orbit does

Orbit brings into one window tools you would usually find in a dozen programs:

  • download videos, playlists and music from hundreds of sites (YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Spotify, Deezer, file hosts…) and follow your favourite channels;
  • convert almost any file: video (accelerated by NVIDIA graphics cards), audio, image, LUT, fonts, 3D models, documents, After Effects presets;
  • enhance and create with AI: generate images, erase or replace an object, remove a background, upscale a video, make it smoother, turn an image into a 3D model, retouch a video shot;
  • work on sound: split vocals from music, denoise, normalize, transcribe and subtitle;
  • organize and share: media library, a toolbox of more than 60 small tools, encrypted cloud storage.

Most AI tools run on your computer: your files never leave your machine, and once the models are downloaded, they work offline.

What you need

Minimum For the AI tools
System Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Memory 8 GB 16 GB or more
Graphics card Any recent card NVIDIA RTX, 8 GB of video memory or more (16 GB for 3D and Paint a plate)
Disk 1 GB for the app From a few GB to more than 60 GB depending on the AI models installed
Internet To download, and for the first install of the models —

Download, conversion and toolbox features work on any PC. The AI tools use the graphics card when there is one; some (AI 3D Creation, Paint a plate, local AI Generation) need an NVIDIA card.
